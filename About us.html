Our Story

Vedic Numero AI was born from a simple yet powerful idea: What if the ancient wisdom of Vedic numerology could be made instantly accessible to everyone through artificial intelligence?

Founded by Prem Aggarwal, Vedic Numero AI is a unique platform that blends spiritual science with modern technology. As the Founder, Developer, and Numerologist, Prem transformed his vision into reality by building an AI-powered system that simplifies complex numerological concepts into easy, personalized insights. With a strong passion for technology and deep interest in numerology, he aims to help people understand their life path, strengths, and purpose in a clear and practical way.

The journey of Vedic Numero AI is strengthened by its core team.
Dev N Suman, Co-Founder and Developer of Vedic Numero AI, brings strong expertise in artificial intelligence and product development. He plays a key role in building and optimizing the platform by integrating advanced AI with numerological systems to deliver accurate, fast, and meaningful insights to users.

Adding depth and authenticity to the platform, (Director) Dr. Prof. Mahesh Sharma, & Co-Founder of Vedic Numero AI, is an expert numerologist with years of knowledge in Vedic numerology. His guidance ensures that the platform stays true to traditional principles while adapting them for the modern digital world. His mentorship has been instrumental in shaping the vision and credibility of Vedic Numero AI.

Together, the team shares a common mission — to democratize Vedic numerology and make it accessible to everyone. By combining ancient wisdom with intelligent technology, NumerAI provides users with personalized guidance to make better decisions, understand relationships, and align with their true potential. Today, Vedic Numero AI is growing as an innovative AI-driven platform, helping people across different backgrounds discover clarity, purpose, and direction in their lives. And this is just the beginning the journey continues to evolve, bringing the power of numbers closer to the world.
